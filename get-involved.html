Get Involved

Join our community of aviation enthusiasts and professionals in the Northern Virginia area. Membership is open to everyone who loves aviation.

Why Join?

Benefits of Membership

Local Networking

Connect with fellow aviators, aerospace professionals, and aviation enthusiasts right here in the Northern Virginia and D.C. area.

WAI Conference Access

As a WAI member, attend the world's largest aviation career conference with thousands of aviation professionals from around the globe.

Scholarship Opportunities

Access WAI's scholarship database and get chapter support for applying to flight training, aviation education, and career development scholarships.

Member Community

Join our closed, safe member platform where chapter members share news, plan events, ask questions, and stay connected between meetings.

Chapter Events

Attend monthly meetings, fly-ins, airport tours, airshows, Girls in Aviation Day, and other chapter-organized events throughout the year.

Mentorship Program

Connect with experienced aviators for one-on-one mentorship, or become a mentor yourself and help shape the next generation of pilots.

Membership

How to Join

Joining WAI-CRC is a two-step process — become a WAI International member, then join our local chapter.

1

Join WAI International

Visit wai.org to create a WAI International membership. Choose the membership level that works for you.

2

Complete Chapter Application

Fill out the WAI-CRC local chapter membership application and pay local dues of $15.00 — billed annually when you initially join and each year thereafter.

3

Welcome to the Chapter!

Once approved, you'll receive a welcome email with details on upcoming chapter events and how to connect with fellow members.

Start Your Application

Questions? Contact us and a chapter leader will get back to you.
