Who We Are

A Community Built on Wings

The WAI Capital Region Chapter (WAI-CRC) is a local chapter of Women In Aviation International — a nonprofit 501(c)(3) dedicated to providing networking, education, mentoring, and scholarship opportunities for women and men in all aviation career fields.

Our members span the Northern Virginia and greater Washington D.C. area, coming together to celebrate aviation, support the next generation of aviators, and honor the legacy of trailblazing women in flight.

What We Do

Our Programs & Focus Areas

From mentorship to scholarships, we support aviation enthusiasts at every stage of their journey.

Education & Scholarships

Supporting aspiring aviators through WAI's scholarship programs and local educational initiatives to help members achieve their flight training goals.

Networking & Mentorship

Connecting aviation professionals, students, and enthusiasts through chapter events, our member community, and the annual WAI conference.

Girls in Aviation Day

An annual event inspiring the next generation of female aviators through hands-on experiences, demonstrations, and stories from women in aviation careers.

WASP Heritage

Honoring the Women Airforce Service Pilots through our interactive gravesite database, preserving the legacy of these pioneering aviators for future generations.

Community Events

From fly-ins to museum tours and airshows, we organize local events that bring together aviation lovers across the greater Washington D.C. area.

Career Development

Helping members advance in their aviation careers through resume workshops, interview prep, and connections to aviation employers in the region.
