Girls in Aviation Day 2025 — Save the Date!

📅 May 15, 2025 ✍️ WAI-CRC Staff

We are thrilled to announce that Girls in Aviation Day 2025 is coming up soon! This annual event, organized by Women In Aviation International chapters worldwide, is dedicated to inspiring young women to pursue careers in aviation and aerospace.

This year's event will feature hands-on activities, meet-and-greets with women pilots and aviation professionals, cockpit tours, and engaging STEM activities for participants of all ages.

What to Expect

  • Interactive flight simulators and cockpit tours
  • Panel discussions with women in aviation careers
  • STEM and aviation activities for kids and teens
  • Scholarship information and mentorship sign-ups
  • Networking with local aviation professionals

WAI Scholarship Applications Now Open

📅 March 10, 2025 ✍️ WAI-CRC Staff

Women In Aviation International has opened applications for its annual scholarship program. WAI offers numerous scholarships to support members pursuing aviation education, flight training, and aerospace careers.

How to Apply

  • Must be a current WAI International member
  • Visit wai.org/scholarships for the full list
  • Scholarships available for pilots, mechanics, ATC, and more
  • Applications typically due in the fall — check the WAI site for deadlines

Chapter members who need help with applications are encouraged to reach out — we're here to support you!

Aviation News

Celebrating Women's History Month in Aviation

📅 March 1, 2025 ✍️ WAI-CRC Staff

March is Women's History Month, and the WAI Capital Region Chapter is celebrating the incredible legacy of women in aviation — from Bessie Coleman and Amelia Earhart to the Women Airforce Service Pilots (WASP) and the trailblazers of today.

Did you know? The WASP (Women Airforce Service Pilots) flew over 60 million miles for the U.S. military during WWII, yet weren't granted full military status until 1977. Our WASP Gravesite Map honors their memory.

Share your story or nominate a woman in aviation who inspires you by contacting us.
