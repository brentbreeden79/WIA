About WAI-CRC

Our story, mission, and the community of aviation enthusiasts behind the Women In Aviation International Capital Region Chapter.

Our Mission

Empowering Aviators at Every Level

The WAI Capital Region Chapter (WAI-CRC) is a nonprofit local chapter of Women In Aviation International — the world's leading organization dedicated to the encouragement and advancement of women in all aviation career fields and interests.

We serve the Northern Virginia and greater Washington D.C. area, bringing together pilots, aerospace engineers, air traffic controllers, flight attendants, mechanics, aviation enthusiasts, and anyone who simply loves to fly.

Our chapter provides a welcoming, inclusive community where all genders are supported in pursuing their aviation dreams — from first flights to career milestones.

WAI International is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization headquartered in Dayton, Ohio, with chapters worldwide. Our Capital Region Chapter serves the greater D.C. / Northern Virginia area.

Our Story

Chapter History

The Beginning

WAI Capital Region Chapter Founded

A group of aviation-passionate women and men in Northern Virginia came together to form a local WAI chapter, establishing a community hub for regional aviators.

Growing Community

First Girls in Aviation Day

The chapter hosted its inaugural Girls in Aviation Day, inspiring dozens of young women to consider careers in flight.

Heritage Project

WASP Gravesite Database Launched

In partnership with Texas Woman's University, the chapter helped bring the WASP gravesite database online with Google Maps integration — honoring the Women Airforce Service Pilots.

Digital Connection

Chapter Moves to Geneva Platform

To better serve members, the chapter adopted Geneva as its closed, safe member communication platform — keeping the community connected year-round.

Today

Continuing the Mission

WAI-CRC continues to grow, welcoming aviators at all levels and expanding programs to serve the Northern Virginia and D.C. aviation community.
